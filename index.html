The Hoek-Brown Formula

• mb = mi corrected × exp((GSI − 100)/(28 − 14D))

• s = exp((GSI − 100)/(9 − 3D))

• a = 0.5 + ⅙ × (e−GSI/15 − e−20/3)

• Rock Mass UCS: σcm = σci × sa (σci = Intact UCS)

• MiCorr = mi × (1-0.4 × DoS/100)
